About Dr B.S. Katiyar

Dr B.S. Katiyar is the founder and chief physician at Punarnava Ayurvedic Hospital, Kanpur's leading centre for Panchakarma and Ksharsutra surgery. With over 20 years of clinical experience in Ayurvedic medicine, Dr Katiyar has treated thousands of patients from across Uttar Pradesh and beyond.

He holds an MD in Ayurvedic Medicine from Lucknow University and a Post Graduate Certificate in Ksharsutra (PGCKS) — making him one of the few specialists in Kanpur formally trained in the ancient Ayurvedic para-surgical technique. He has been a practitioner in Neuro Yoga, Panchakarma, and Ksharsutra surgery throughout his career.

Specialisations

  • Ksharsutra Surgery — for piles (all grades), anal fissure, fistula-in-ano, pilonidal sinus
  • Panchakarma — Vaman, Virechan, Basti, Nasya, Raktamokshan
  • Poorva Karma — Snehan (oil therapy), Swedan (steam therapy)
  • Nadi Pariksha (pulse diagnosis)
  • Shiro-Dhara therapy
  • Anorectal disease management
  • Chronic disease management through Ayurveda (diabetes, obesity, joint pain)

Qualifications & Training

  • MD — Ayurvedic Medicine, Lucknow University, Uttar Pradesh
  • PGCKS — Post Graduate Certificate in Ksharsutra Surgery
  • Specialist training in Neuro Yoga and Panchakarma therapy
