## Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

### C1 — Zero Schema Markup Across All Pages
**Pages affected:** All 8 pages  
No schema.org markup exists anywhere on the site. AI systems cannot identify the business as a medical entity, cannot read doctor credentials, cannot understand services, and cannot generate rich snippets. This is the single biggest barrier to AI visibility.  
**Fix:** Add `LocalBusiness` + `MedicalClinic` + `Physician` schema (see Week 1 plan).

### C2 — No sitemap.xml
**Status:** 404 Not Found  
AI crawlers and search engines have no way to discover all pages. Pages like `typeofpooravkarma.html` and `pradhankarma.html` may never be indexed.  
**Fix:** Generate and submit a sitemap immediately.

### C3 — No Meta Descriptions on 7 of 8 Pages
**Pages affected:** about-us, services, ksharsutra, contact, poorva-karma, pradhan-karma, paschatya-karma  
Without meta descriptions, search engines write them automatically — usually poorly. AI systems use meta descriptions as the first signal for page relevance.  
**Fix:** Write unique 150–160 character meta descriptions for every page.

### C4 — NAP Inconsistency (Multiple Postal Codes)
**Variants found:**  
- Homepage footer: `208025`  
- Contact page: `208024`  
- Google Business Profile: `208024`  

Google and AI systems use NAP consistency as a trust signal. Inconsistencies suppress local rankings.  
**Fix:** Standardise to `208024` (matching Google Business Profile) across all pages.

### C5 — Contact Page Title Spelling Error
**Title found:** `"Contatct Us"`  
Typos in `<title>` tags hurt trust signals with both crawlers and AI systems.  
**Fix:** Change to `"Contact Us — Punarnava Ayurvedic Hospital, Kanpur"`.

## High Priority Issues

### H1 — No llms.txt File
AI models that actively browse the web (Perplexity, ChatGPT with browsing, Gemini) look for `/llms.txt` to understand a site's structure and permissions. Its absence means no optimised AI-specific entry point.  
**Fix:** Create `/llms.txt` with hospital description, services, and key page links (see Week 1 plan).

### H2 — No robots.txt
A missing robots.txt is treated ambiguously by crawlers. While it doesn't actively block them, it signals an unmanaged site. All major AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) should be explicitly welcomed.  
**Fix:** Create `robots.txt` explicitly allowing AI crawlers + pointing to sitemap.

### H3 — No Question-Answering Content / FAQ Blocks
**Pages affected:** All pages  
AI systems are optimised to extract Q&A content. Searches like *"Is Ksharsutra treatment safe?"* or *"How long does Panchakarma treatment take in Kanpur?"* currently return no content from pahindia.com because no FAQ blocks exist.  
**Fix:** Add FAQ sections to every service page (10+ questions per page).

### H4 — No Open Graph / Social Meta Tags
Sharing any page on WhatsApp, Facebook, or LinkedIn shows no image, no title, no description — a blank link preview. This reduces click-through and social signals.  
**Fix:** Add `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, `og:url` to all pages.

### H5 — Not Listed on Practo or Sulekha
These are the two biggest Indian healthcare discovery platforms. Being absent means missing patients who search for Ayurvedic doctors on these platforms — and missing the citations AI models draw from them.  
**Fix:** Create verified profiles on Practo and Sulekha this week.
